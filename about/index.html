About Emmanuel Effiom Duke

Emmanuel Effiom Duke (also known as Emmanuel Duke) is a Nigerian-trained mechanical engineer, AI engineer, researcher and technology entrepreneur based in Enugu State, Nigeria. He is the founder of Dukers LTD and the creator of Teger AI.

Background and education

Emmanuel earned his Bachelor of Engineering in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka in 2024, with a grounding in thermodynamics, energy systems and applied engineering that still shapes his research. Earlier, he received the Agbami Medical & Engineering Scholarship (NNPC/Chevron Joint Venture, 2018).

From January 2022 to January 2023 he trained as a student maintenance intern at Transcorp Hotels Plc in Calabar, working on the operation and maintenance of MEP and HVAC systems, including air-cooled chillers, boilers and elevators. In 2024 he was selected for the Politecnico di Torino Earth Summer School, which explored hybrid technologies that convert CO₂ into commercially valuable products. He also completed Imperial College London's Introduction to Air Quality.

AI engineering and AI safety

Emmanuel builds AI products with security at their core. Teger AI, which he created, is an explainable social-engineering defence layer for suspicious email and chat messages. Its Chrome extension scans user-selected Gmail and Slack messages and detects manipulation tactics against a shared threat taxonomy. It then explains the concrete cues behind each warning and recommends a safe next action. Message content is treated as untrusted, the extension never holds provider API keys, and scan history is stored as metadata only. Teger AI is at MVP v0.2 in private beta.

Alongside product work he runs open, reproducible mechanistic-interpretability research:

  • SPD Mechanism Clustering: a systematic empirical study of automated mechanism clustering for Stochastic Parameter Decomposition. Its seven experiments run from toy models to a real grokked transformer.
  • Deception Feature Universality: a cross-architecture study that found statistically significant representational similarity for deception cues (CKA 0.56 vs a 0.26 null, p = 0.024).
  • Reward-Generalization Early Warning: tests whether internal signals can flag unintended reward generalisation before it shows up in outputs.
  • mechinterp-phishing-probe: probes for social-engineering circuits in language models.

In 2026 his Apart Sprints entry, Four False Positives and One Real Signal: A Controlled Audit of Secret-Loyalty Organisms, placed in the top half of 179 submissions at the Secret Loyalties Hackathon. He has completed AI Safety Hungary's AI Safety Fundamentals course and its Technical AI Safety / Alignment track.

Software engineering and cybersecurity

Emmanuel ships full-stack products across fintech, agriculture, geospatial and security. AxiosPay, a cross-border FX app for African travellers, placed 4th at the Interswitch × Enyata Developer Hackathon. His other public projects include Cropie (crop-disease early warning), SoilIQ (soil-health intelligence), FarmVoice-AI (an agricultural extension agent), GeoDrill (drilling-probability heatmaps) and LandChain Africa (a land registry with GIS boundary-dispute resolution). He holds Cisco's IT Essentials certification. His public code is on GitHub.

Engineering and energy research

He took part in the EU Erasmus+ AirForAll citizen-testing group (2023–2024), and completed Politecnico di Milano's off-grid energy systems modelling course (MICROGRIDSPY) and Strathmore University's Data for Renewable Energy Systems course (both 2025). See the full list on Google Scholar.
