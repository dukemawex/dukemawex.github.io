SPD Mechanism Clustering

Mechanistic-interpretability research: the first systematic empirical study of automated mechanism clustering for Stochastic Parameter Decomposition (SPD). Seven experiments from toy models to a real grokked transformer, showing component specialization — not clustering-signal choice — is the binding constraint. Paper + reproducible code.

About

AI, engineering & entrepreneurship.

I'm Emmanuel Duke (Emmanuel Effiom Duke), an AI builder, mechanical engineer and technology entrepreneur. I hold a B.Eng. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka, and I'm the founder of Dukers LTD.

AI engineering and safety. I build AI products such as Teger AI, which targets psychological manipulation in messages. I also run mechanistic-interpretability research on how models represent deception and reward, published as open, reproducible code.

Software and cybersecurity. I ship full-stack products in fintech, agriculture, geospatial and security. They include AxiosPay, a cross-border FX app that placed 4th at the Interswitch × Enyata Developer Hackathon.

Engineering research. My mechanical-engineering work covers CFD modelling of steam-plant feedwater heaters, machine-learning energy optimisation and air-quality management, presented at international conferences.

Community. I mentor African founders through the Tony Elumelu Foundation and lead growth for The Upskill Space, a youth digital-skills academy.
