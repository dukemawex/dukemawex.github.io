On 4 November 2026, Emmanuel Effiom Duke was the keynote speaker at Empowering The Next Generation: Promoting the Ethical Use of AI Among University Students, organised by the TCAF Initiative at NSLT 1, University of Ilorin, in Ilorin, Kwara State, Nigeria. His talk, Empowering with Integrity: Navigating AI, Academic Responsibility and the Future of Learning, addressed ethical AI use, academic integrity and how students can keep authorship while using AI tools.

Emmanuel Effiom Duke is an AI engineer, researcher and founder of Dukers LTD (products include Teger AI and Transly). Photos from the keynote are also on the homepage.
